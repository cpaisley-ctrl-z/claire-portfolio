My name is Claire. I am a designer and a dreamer.

II dream of a world where the internet is fun, accessible, and honest. I strive for those values to show up in my UX design work.

I recently graduated from Pratt Institute with a Master of Science in Information Experience Design. I have previously led freelance UX research and design projects for clients such as The Cooper Hewitt Smithsonian Design Museum, The National Gallery of Art, WNYC, and Gutenberg Technology.

When I’m not hanging out on the computer you’ll find me hiking, biking, or shooting film on my dad’s old 35mm camera.
